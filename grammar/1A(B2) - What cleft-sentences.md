## Core idea

**What-cleft sentences** are used to give extra focus to one part of the sentence.

They are common when we want to:
- highlight the most important information
- sound more emphatic
- organise spoken or written explanations

```text
I’ve found it’s just a great way to get rid of all those frustrations from work.
→ What I’ve found is that it’s just a great way to get rid of all those frustrations from work.
```

## Main patterns

### 1. What + clause + be + focus

Use this pattern to highlight the key information.

| Case | Form | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| focus on an idea / statement | what + clause + be + focus | **What I’ve found is** that it’s just a great way to get rid of all those frustrations from work. |
| focus on the thing that caused a reaction | what + clause + be + focus | **What put me off was** the pain. |
| focus on what is especially good | what + clause + be + focus | **What is great about it is** the way they make it relevant to now. |
| focus on what you love | what + clause + be + focus | **What I love is** how friendly people are. |

### 2. What + clause + be + the thing/fact/reason

Use this version when you want the sentence to sound more explicit or formal.

| Case | Form | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| focus on an important detail | what + clause + be + the fact that ... | **What is great about this class is the fact that** no one takes it too seriously. |
| focus on explanation | what + clause + be + the reason why ... | **What matters is the reason why** they changed the rules. |

## Which form to use

| Meaning | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| выделить, что ты понял / заметил | what + subject + verb + be ... | **What I’ve found is** that it’s a great way to relax. |
| выделить причину реакции | what + verb + object + be ... | **What put me off was** the pain. |
| выделить лучший аспект | what + be + adjective + about ... + be ... | **What is great about it is** the atmosphere. |
| выделить любимую часть | what + subject + love/like/hate + be ... | **What I love is** the people. |
| выделить факт | what + clause + be + that-clause | **What is important is** that everyone feels included. |

## Common transformations

| Normal sentence | What-cleft sentence |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| I’ve found it’s just a great way to get rid of all those frustrations from work. | **What I’ve found is** that it’s just a great way to get rid of all those frustrations from work. |
| The pain put me off. | **What put me off was** the pain. |
| The way they make it relevant to now is great. | **What is great about it is** the way they make it relevant to now. |
| I love how friendly people are. | **What I love is** how friendly people are. |
| No one in this class takes it too seriously, which is great. | **What is great about this class is** that no one takes it too seriously. |
| The best thing about the course is the teacher. | **What is best about the course is** the teacher. |

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Correct | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| What I’ve found it is that it’s useful. | **What I’ve found is** that it’s useful. | Do not repeat `it` after the what-clause. |
| What put me off is the pain. | **What put me off was** the pain. | Use the natural past form here because the original action was in the past. |
| What I love how friendly people are. | **What I love is** how friendly people are. | A what-cleft needs `be`. |
| What is great this class is that no one takes it too seriously. | **What is great about this class is** that no one takes it too seriously. | Use `about` after `great` in this structure. |

## Quick choice guide

| If you want to say... | Use... | Form |
|-----------------------|---------|------|
| что ты понял / заметил | what-cleft | what + clause + be + focus |
| что именно тебя оттолкнуло | what-cleft | what + verb + object + be + focus |
| что тебе особенно нравится | what-cleft | what + subject + love/like + be + focus |
| что именно хорошо в чём-то | what-cleft | what + be + adjective + about + noun + be + focus |
| выделить целый факт | what-cleft | what + clause + be + that-clause |
